### Bill C-22: Key Provisions in Plain Language

**Section 5(2)(a)** gives the government the power to require electronic service providers to develop, implement, test, maintain, and operate technical capabilities that allow authorized authorities to extract, organize, and provide access to information that they are legally permitted to obtain.

**Section 5(2)(b)** allows the government to require providers to install, use, operate, manage, test, and maintain devices, equipment, or other technologies that may enable authorized persons to access information.

**Section 7(1)** allows the Minister to impose these same obligations directly on individual providers through ministerial orders, without needing to pass a new law each time, as long as the obligations are authorized under section 5(2).

**Why these sections are debated:** Critics argue that these powers are broad enough that future regulations or orders could require companies to redesign systems or add technical capabilities that weaken encryption or facilitate government access. The government disagrees and points to other provisions in the bill, including section 5(5), which says providers cannot be required to introduce a "systemic vulnerability."
